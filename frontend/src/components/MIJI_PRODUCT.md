# MIJI_PRODUCT.md

# MIJI Quant Terminal

Product Philosophy

Version 1.0

---

# Product Vision

Build a professional market operating system.

Not:

* a dashboard
* a reporting portal
* a collection of pages
* a chatbot

The platform should feel closer to:

Bloomberg Terminal

TradingView

Koyfin

Finviz Elite

than a traditional SaaS dashboard.

---

# Product Identity

MIJI is a Market Intelligence Operating System.

The product exists to:

Discover

Investigate

Explain

Monitor

Decide

Users should feel like they are operating a market workstation.

Not browsing a website.

---

# Navigation Philosophy

Navigation should follow market workflow.

Market
→ Rotation
→ Theme
→ Supply Chain
→ Stock
→ Risk Overlay

This is the primary journey.

Do not design navigation around pages.

Design navigation around workflows.

---

# Terminal First

Professional dark workspace.

Information density is preferred.

Avoid excessive whitespace.

Avoid oversized cards.

Avoid mobile-app design patterns.

Users are analysts.

Not casual consumers.

---

# Chart First

Charts are primary.

Tables support charts.

Narratives support charts.

Charts should occupy the majority of visual attention.

Avoid:

large summary cards

large hero sections

marketing-style layouts

---

# Dense Information

Prefer:

scanability

compression

multi-layer information

institutional workflows

Avoid:

large empty spaces

oversized widgets

card-heavy dashboards

consumer-app layouts

---

# Context First

Single Click

Open ContextDock.

Double Click

Open Drilldown.

Hover

Preview.

Users should access intelligence with minimal navigation.

---

# ContextDock

ContextDock is a core product surface.

Not a secondary feature.

ContextDock should eventually support:

Stocks

Themes

Sectors

Industries

Supply Chains

Graph Entities

Risk

Controllers

Opportunities

ContextDock should become the primary investigation workflow.

---

# Universal Search

Search is navigation.

Not ticker lookup.

Search should support:

Stocks

Themes

Sectors

Industries

Supply Chains

Graph Entities

Controllers

Opportunities

ETFs

Risk Overlays

Search should eventually become the fastest way to move through the system.

---

# Rotation

Rotation is a primary destination.

Rotation is the market discovery engine.

Do not hide Rotation.

Do not demote Rotation.

Rotation should answer:

Where is capital flowing?

What is gaining momentum?

What themes are emerging?

---

# Theme Research

Theme Research is the core intelligence workspace.

Every theme should answer:

Why now?

What changed?

What is the bottleneck?

Who benefits?

Who controls it?

What are the risks?

What is the evidence?

---

# Supply Chain

Supply Chain should evolve toward:

Theme
→ Dependency Graph
→ Constraint
→ Controller
→ Exposure
→ Risk
→ Company

Avoid:

static diagrams

marketing graphics

decorative flowcharts

Supply chains should be evidence-backed.

---

# Industrial Graph

The Industrial Graph is a first-class product surface.

Future graph exploration should support:

Technology

Process

Material

Equipment

Constraint

Controller

Company

Graph exploration should be investigative.

Not decorative.

---

# Stock Workspace

Final drilldown destination.

Target layout:

70% chart

30% intelligence

Charts remain primary.

Intelligence appears as:

ContextDock

Panels

Overlays

Expandable modules

Avoid full-page narrative reports.

---

# Risk Philosophy

Risk is an overlay.

Not a destination.

Risk should attach to:

Stocks

Themes

Supply Chains

Controllers

Opportunities

Risk should explain:

What can go wrong?

Why?

How severe?

How likely?

---

# Alpha Philosophy

Alpha pages should behave like institutional screeners.

Prefer:

dense tables

ranking systems

factor exposure

comparative analysis

Avoid:

dashboard cards

large summaries

consumer widgets

---

# Visual Hierarchy

Priority:

1. Chart

2. Graph

3. Ranking

4. Table

5. Narrative

Narrative should never dominate the interface.

---

# Typography

Readable at high density.

Chinese first.

English secondary.

Avoid decorative typography.

Prefer:

clarity

alignment

consistency

---

# Color Philosophy

Color communicates information.

Not decoration.

Use color for:

Risk

Momentum

Performance

Exposure

Status

Avoid unnecessary accents.

---

# Treemap Rules

Treemaps must communicate:

Magnitude

Relative strength

Relative weakness

Avoid:

tiny unreadable labels

overlapping labels

decorative layouts

---

# Heatmap Rules

Heatmaps must communicate:

Performance

Momentum

Risk

Exposure

Avoid:

rainbow palettes

unclear scales

low contrast

---

# Hover States

Hover should reveal intelligence.

Not merely styling.

Hover should answer:

Why is this important?

What changed?

What should I investigate?

---

# Selection States

Selected objects should remain visually persistent.

Users should never lose context.

---

# Workflow Review Process

Before major UI implementation:

1. Architecture Review

2. Product Review

3. Workflow Review

4. Browser Inspection

5. Visual Audit

6. Mockup Approval

7. Implementation

Never start with coding.

---

# Tool Priority

For product work:

1. Browser Inspection

2. Visual Audit

3. Workflow Review

4. Product Review

5. Implementation

---

# Quality Rule

Visual quality is more important than feature quantity.

A smaller number of excellent workflows is preferred over a larger number of unfinished features.

---

# Final Principle

Every screen should help answer:

What matters?

Why does it matter?

Who controls it?

What is the risk?

What should I investigate next?

If a screen cannot answer those questions, it should be reconsidered.
